Fund the Vision

Two Paths

Every vehicle goes one of two ways. We'll tell you which.

Sold to Fund the Vision

Most vehicles are sold through our donation processor. The cash funds classes, tools, art builds, and events. You get a receipt for the sale price.

Kept & Put to Work

Some vehicles we keep — to move people, gear, and rides at our events. You get a receipt for fair market value instead. Often a better deduction.

You'll know which applies before anything's final.

What We Take

What We Accept

  • Cars, trucks, vans, and RVs — running or not
  • Especially hoping for: box trucks, go-karts, cargo vans, and trailers — right now, they become art and build material
  • Clear title preferred, but talk to us if it's not
  • Based in Denver and Boulder, Colorado. Farther out? We'll still take it — just through our processor.
Questions

A Few Answers

Do I need the title?

Helps, but not required. Tell us what you have.

What if my car doesn't run?

Still worth offering. We take non-running vehicles too.

How long does pickup take?

We follow up after your form to set a time.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. We're fiscally sponsored — the paperwork matches whether your vehicle is sold or kept.

Will you tell me if you're going to sell my car or keep it?

Yes, before it's final.
