The Menu

What We Teach

Half the menu needs only the open floor. The trades bring their own tables and pack away.

How It Works

How Classes Run

We open with four classes, not all fourteen, and add more as the rooms fill.

i

Four-week series

One night a week, two hours a night. Week four ends with a showing at that month's Society Night, so every student has a reason to come back and bring friends.

ii

Drop-ins welcome

Anyone can take a single class. Members on Guild Dues take every class.

iii

Small rooms

About twelve students for movement classes and eight for trades, so everyone gets real time with the teacher.

iv

Taught by guild members

Each class has a named teacher and a named person who sets up and leaves the room as we found it.

v

Price

Single Class $25. Guild Dues and Guild Dues+ cover every class as a monthly membership.

The Room

Care for the Space

The floor stays clean: messy trades go on drop cloths, and nothing with fire runs indoors.

What we'd need

  • Storage for a few bins of props, stilts and paint between classes
  • Folding tables, or room to bring our own
  • A sink for brushes after sign painting

Questions for you

  • Which nights are free, and can we use daytime hours?
  • How high is the ceiling? Stilts need about ten feet.
  • Do you have crash mats for acrobatics?
  • Would any of your teachers co-teach Contact Meets Clown?
  • Any floor-care rules we should know (shoes, paint, tape)?
